About Our Firm

Your Trusted Partner
Since 2000

Chetan Agarwal & Co. is a distinguished Chartered Accountancy firm rooted in Jamnagar, Gujarat. Founded in 2000 with a commitment to professional excellence, we have grown into a trusted name across the region.

With offices in Jamnagar and Ahmedabad, we serve a diverse clientele — from individual taxpayers and MSMEs to large corporations — providing comprehensive solutions in taxation, audit, compliance, and business advisory.

Our approach is simple: understand your unique needs, deliver accurate and timely solutions, and maintain the highest standards of professional conduct and confidentiality.
